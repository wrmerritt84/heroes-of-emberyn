On a Mac

Download for Mac
  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Heroes of Emberyn into your Applications folder.
  2. Double-click it. Your Mac will refuse and say Apple could not verify it. Press Done. Never press Move to Trash.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and press Open Anyway next to Heroes of Emberyn. Confirm with your fingerprint or password.
  4. Open it again. It works from then on, and never asks again.
Your Mac is not saying the game is unsafe. It is saying nobody has paid Apple to vouch for it, which is true: it was made at a kitchen table. Once per computer.

On an Android phone or tablet

Download for Android
  1. Open this page on the phone and press the button.
  2. Chrome will ask whether to allow installing apps from it. Say yes.
  3. Open the file when it finishes.

Already have it?

You do not need this page. Open the game and it tells you when there is a newer version, with a button that does the whole thing for you. Your cards are in your Google Drive rather than in the app, so updating never risks them.
