Heroes of Emberyn

The lamp is lit. Welcome, Adventurers!

A card game set in Emberyn, made at a kitchen table for one family and shared with anyone who wants it. Two players sit across a wooden table in an inn called Chandler's Rest and play thirty cards each: knights, dwarves, elves, dragons, hedgehog engineers and a guild of cats who steal things.

Every card you play grows. Draw it, play it, win with it, and it climbs from Standard through to Mythic, gaining stats, keywords and eventually a new name. Your collection is yours and it follows you.

How it plays

Resources never ramp. You get six every turn from turn one, so nobody is ever stuck watching an opponent play while they wait. Which kinds of six you get is decided by the deck you built.

Light is the gate instead. Your candle rises by one each turn, and a card needs at least its own Light to come down. That is what stops a turn-one dragon.

No dice. The only randomness is the shuffle. No card ever picks a random target.

Playing together

The game plays on its own with no internet at all. To play each other, one computer hosts a table on the local wifi and everyone else joins it with a short room code. There is no matchmaking, no lobby full of strangers, and no server of ours in the middle.

Where your cards live

If you sign in with Google, your collection is kept in your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder that only this game can see. It follows you to any computer, phone or tablet you sign in on. We do not keep a copy, because we do not run a server. See the privacy policy for exactly what that means.

Getting it

Install the game, for a Mac or for an Android phone or tablet. No computer at home needs to be switched on for that: the downloads live here.

If you already have it, you do not need that page. The game tells you when there is a newer version and updates itself.
