Privacy

Short, because there is very little to say.

Last updated 21 September 2026

Heroes of Emberyn has no server. There is nowhere for us to collect anything to, and we do not. No accounts of ours, no database, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.

What is stored, and where

The game stores one thing: your player. That is the name and mark you chose, the cards you own, the decks you built, your record of wins and losses, and the Feats the game keeps about your games.

It is stored in two places, both of which belong to you:

  • On the device you are playing on, so the game works with no internet at all.
  • In your own Google Drive, if you choose to sign in with Google. It goes into the hidden per-application folder that Google provides for this purpose. This game can see only the file it created there. It cannot see your documents, your photos, or anything else in your Drive, and it never asks for permission to.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. The game is fully playable without it.

If you do sign in, you are signed in on Google's own page, in your own browser. The game never sees your password. Google gives the game two things: permission to use that hidden folder, and your email address, which is shown on screen so you can tell which account your cards are saved to. Your email address is kept on your own device and is not sent anywhere.

Playing over local wifi

When people play each other, one device hosts and the others join over the local network. Names, decks and the state of the game pass between those devices directly. Nothing about those games leaves the network you are on.

Children

This game is played by adults and children alike, which is exactly why it is built the way it is: there is no account to create with us, no message system, no other players except the ones in the room, and no data collected about anybody. The only information that exists is a list of cards, and it sits in the player's own Google account.

Removing your information

You can take away the game's access at any time from your Google Account permissions. Doing so deletes the hidden folder and everything in it. To remove the copy on a device, delete the app.

Changes
